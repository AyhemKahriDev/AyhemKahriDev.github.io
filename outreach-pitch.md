# Sponsor outreach — send-ready messages

For spielkunst, the free browser arcade at <https://spielkunst.pages.dev/>.
Full rate card, payment terms and process: [`docs/SPONSORSHIP.md`](docs/SPONSORSHIP.md).

**Read before sending**

- Max **five messages a week**, one per prospect, personalised. No BCC, no bulk lists.
- Never claim traffic or impressions — there is no analytics by design. Say the honest
  thing: "real, small, growing with the daily puzzle".
- Offer the launch price ($5 for one week) for the first month, then $15 / month per
  category or $35 / month site-wide.
- Payment is an invoice after the slot is live — bank transfer or Payoneer. No payment
  link, no platform, no card details in the first message.
- Target one sponsor per category: word, puzzle, arcade, brain, strategy, sandbox, classics,
  racing, table. Never two in the same category.

## Where to find prospects (pick, don't scrape)

- Authors of games already in the catalogue who have their own paid game or tool.
- Puzzle-book sellers on Gumroad/Etsy with a word-search or sudoku product.
- Small newsletters about games, education, or family screen-time.
- Teachers' resource blogs and printable-worksheet shops.
- Indie dev-tool makers (level editors, asset packs, HTML5 game templates).
