Memory Match — play free online

A free memory match game. Flip two cards, remember where the pairs hide, and clear the board in as few moves as possible. Play free in your browser on mobile or desktop.

How to play Memory Match

  • Tap a card to flip it over.
  • Tap a second card. If they match, both stay revealed.
  • If they do not match, they turn back over - remember where they were.
  • Clear every pair in as few moves as you can.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play memory match?

Tap one card to flip it, then tap a second. Matching pairs stay face up, mismatched pairs flip back after a moment. Clear the whole board in the fewest moves.

Is Memory Match free on arcadefork?

Yes - completely free, no account and no download. Your best score is saved on your device.

How many cards are in the game?

A 4x4 board of 16 cards on normal difficulty. Try easy or hard mode with the difficulty switch to change the board size.
