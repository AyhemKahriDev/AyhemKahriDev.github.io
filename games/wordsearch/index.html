Daily Word Search — play free online

A free daily word search puzzle. Every player gets the same 10x10 grid, the clock starts at 100 seconds, and your score is based on the words you find and how fast you find them. Free to play, no download.

How to play Daily Word Search

  • Drag across a straight line of letters - horizontal, vertical or diagonal.
  • Release to submit the word. Correct words stay highlighted in colour.
  • You have 100 seconds. Longer words and faster finds are worth more points.
  • A new grid is generated every day and everyone plays the same one.

Frequently asked questions

Is the daily word search free?

Yes. Every game on arcadefork is free to play in your browser. There is no account, no download and no paywall.

Do I get a new puzzle every day?

Yes. The grid is generated from the date, so every player worldwide gets exactly the same puzzle, and it changes at midnight UTC.

How is the score calculated?

Each word is worth 60 points plus 20 per letter, doubled by the seconds still left on the clock when you find it.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The game is built mobile first - drag with your finger, and it works in portrait or landscape.
